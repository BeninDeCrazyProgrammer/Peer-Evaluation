How a round works

  1. Set it up

    Create a course, upload the group list, and write your own criteria and rating scale.

  2. Share one link

    Send the link or QR code. Students pick their name, set a 4-digit PIN, and rate their groupmates.

  3. Read the results

    See totals, rankings, a full who-rated-whom matrix, and who never submitted. Export to Excel.

Why ask the group?

The workload splits unevenly but the grade doesn't, and the group notices. Students already know who did what. A shared rubric turns that knowledge into numbers one lecturer can compare across a class, instead of a pile of complaints after the semester ends.

Every member rates every other member, so one grudge or one friend can't skew a result. One submission per student, enforced by the database.
