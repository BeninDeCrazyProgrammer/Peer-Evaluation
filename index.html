PeerEval
Group peer evaluation, online

Evaluations without the paper

A single group grade can't tell you who carried the project and who coasted on it. PeerEval gives a course a fast, structured way to ask the people who'd actually know — the group itself — and turns their answers into numbers a lecturer can act on.

Why bother asking the group?

Every lecturer who assigns group work runs into the same problem eventually: the workload splits unevenly, but the grade doesn't. The student who wrote the report and the student who missed three meetings walk away with the same mark, and the rest of the group notices. By the time it reaches a lecturer as a complaint, the semester is usually over.

Students already know who did the work — they're the only ones in the room for every missed deadline and rescued deliverable. Peer evaluation just gives that knowledge somewhere to go, with a rubric instead of a rumor.

What makes it worth trusting

A stack of anonymous complaints isn't evidence. A fixed set of criteria and a shared rating scale — the same ones for the whole class — mean one student's "3" means roughly what another's "3" means. Every member rates every other member, not just themselves, so a single grudge or a single fan doesn't skew the picture. What comes out the other end is a total per student, a full matrix of who rated whom, and a clear record of who never submitted anything at all — enough to actually weigh a grade against, or enough to know one shouldn't be adjusted at all.

Built around how a course actually runs

One course, several cohorts

A course taught to more than one year group or section gets a separate class for each — its own roster, its own evaluations, never mixed up with another's.

Your rubric, your scale

Criteria and rating scale are set per evaluation, not fixed by the software. Match whatever rubric the department already uses.

Nothing for students to install

A link or QR code, their name or ID, and a PIN they set themselves the first time. No accounts, no app, no password for anyone else to reset.
